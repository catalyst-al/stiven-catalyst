# Page titles fitted for search results (audit SEO-08, 9 November 2026)

A search result cuts a title off at about 60 to 70 characters. `lib/fit-title.js` fits the `<title>` of every page to
65 characters; the heading on the page and the social preview keep the full title. An issue or a volume keeps its
topic and a short number; otherwise the longest beginning that ends at a colon, a comma or a dash and stays within 65
characters (a list is not cut after its first item); a word cut with an ellipsis only where nothing else fits.
Generated from the build before the change; a page added later is fitted the same way.
