## How the register was decided (P4-01)

Every German reflection and insight was scanned for du, dich, dir, dein… outside quotation marks, and every hit was
read against the Albanian original at the same line (the three languages are paragraph-aligned).

- **"Sie"** where the narrator speaks to the reader: as the employee ("Sie sitzen endlich zu Hause"), as the owner or
  manager ("Wenn Sie den Boten der Wahrheit bestrafen"), in questions to the reader, and in imperatives ("Versuchen
  Sie einmal"). Pieces written entirely in "du" (*Einseitige Erreichbarkeit*, *Integrität im Dunkeln*, *Die Kunst,
  Nein zu sagen*, *Die Opferfalle*) are now entirely in "Sie", including their titles and summaries.
- **"man" / impersonal** where the Albanian "ti" is generic, a truth about anyone, not an address: "Armut zwingt
  einen, den Kopf zu senken", "Je mehr man gibt, desto mehr wird von einem verlangt", "Bevor man losrennt, muss man
  wissen…", "die eigene Entscheidung". Also where the German sentence already used "man" just before.
- **"du" kept** in speech: everything inside „…“ (a colleague, the owner, the reader's own questions to a boss keep
  their register), and eight unquoted passages that are speech, listed with reasons in `tests/register.test.cjs`:
  the boss's reply and the trainer's words in italics, the whisper of fear, the interrogation of an employee, the
  organisation's unspoken message in *Das Regelwerk vor dem Menschen*, words to a former colleague, and the author
  speaking to himself (two insights).
- "Lassen Sie mich …" was kept everywhere; it was already "Sie". Sentence order, content and paragraphing are
  unchanged. Verb agreement was checked after each change (one slip of my own, "gesessen hast", was caught and fixed,
  row 199).
- The test fails on any new narrator "du" in `src/content/de/{reflections,insights}`, fails if an allowed passage
  disappears (so the list cannot rot), and checks itself on two sample sentences.

  script that pairs marks per line, skips the front matter, and changes nothing on a line with an odd number of marks
  (there were none). Apostrophes are untouched. One quotation inside a quotation became ‘…’ (row 214).
