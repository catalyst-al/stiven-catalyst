The philosophy behind the work

Clarity into action.

Behind the work

Stiven Janaqi

Operations & leadership · Last mile · Hotels

Stiven has worked in operations for more than ten years, about eight of them leading teams: in retail and wholesale in Albania, then in last-mile logistics and hotels in Germany. In Albania he ran stores and a wholesale business and built a national network of 61 active resellers. In Germany he started again from the ground up, as a parcel courier and a night auditor, and worked his way back into leadership.

As Area Manager for last-mile operations in 2025 he led four shift leads and eight dispatchers, with about 250 drivers on the road. Since November 2025 he has been Night Manager at a Radisson Group hotel in Frankfurt, and since January 2026 he has also coordinated more than 80 vehicles from home, as a part-time last-mile dispatcher.

He has seen how a missed handoff, an unclear standard or a rushed shift changes the number on a dashboard. He writes from that practical perspective and builds small tools that help people see the work, ask better questions and make the next shift easier to run.

STIVEN CATALYST Clarity · Insight · Action · Change

The point is clarity. The catalyst is movement.

Stiven Catalyst exists to turn observation into insight, insight into action and action into progress.

The mark at the heart of the identity is the full stop of “Management without theatre.”, with a ring drawn round it by hand. The point is the moment a complex problem becomes clear: said, done, no performance. The ring is the pen mark a manager makes on a report, a circle round the number that matters. But clarity alone is not enough. The meaning of Catalyst is what follows: creating the conditions for an idea, a person, a team or a system to move forward.

The colours are few on purpose. Black gives the work discipline and seriousness; white creates space to think. Blue is the one accent: clarity, analysis and perspective, and the colour of the point. Gold is reserved for small moments of value and emphasis.

PeopleResults are created by people working inside real systems.
SystemsGood performance should be repeatable, not dependent on heroics.
ChangeAn idea becomes valuable when it changes behaviour or outcomes.

See clearly. Think differently. Act deliberately.

The publication explores leadership, operations, hospitality, logistics, service, accountability, process design and the everyday realities of managing people. It is also an archive of tools and systems built to make work clearer and more reliable.
